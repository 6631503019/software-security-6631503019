5. Forge Ahead (Broken JWT) - 15 pts
Flag: {
  "item": "Phone",
  "note": "FLAG{idor_demo}",
  "owner": "bob",
  "total": 800
}

Payload:
Forge a JWT with:
{"sub":"admin"}

Method:
alg:none OR HS256 using the weak secret if accepted by the assigned target.

Then send:
Authorization: Bearer <FORGED_TOKEN>

Mitigation:
Pin the JWT algorithm, use a strong random signing secret, and validate required claims such as exp and aud before trusting the token.
